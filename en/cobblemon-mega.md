# How to Mega Evolve in Cobblemon

Plain Cobblemon 1.7.3 can't Mega Evolve. The mod has Mega form data but no Mega Bracelet and no Mega Stones. On Coco Cobblemon, Mega comes from the **Mega Showdown** mod: wear a **Mega Bracelet**, give the Pokémon its **Mega Stone**, then press the **Mega** button when you pick a move in battle. You can only have 1 Pokémon in Mega form at a time.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

## Steps to Mega Evolve

1. Get a **Mega Bracelet** and put it in one of the accessory slots (Accessories) in your inventory.
2. Get the right Mega Stone for the species, like Charizardite X for Charizard or Greninjite for Greninja. On Coco Cobblemon they're sold in Shop Tokens (Menu > Shop > Shop Tokens).
3. Give the stone to the Pokémon: send it out, hold **Shift**, right-click it and choose **Change held item**.
4. In battle, when it's time to pick a move, press the **Mega** button, then pick the move. The Pokémon Mega Evolves that turn.

Mega Showdown's default Mega Bracelet recipe (crafting table):

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
|---|---|---|
| Iron Ingot | Diamond | Iron Ingot |
| White Apricorn | Keystone | White Apricorn |
| Iron Ingot | Iron Ingot | Iron Ingot |

This is the default recipe from the mod file; we haven't checked it on the server. Keystones drop from **Keystone Ore**, found in the underground **Megaroid** structure (going by the mod's data). The bracelet also comes in 6 other colours: red, pink, yellow, green, blue and black.

## No Mega button?

- You're not wearing a Mega Bracelet, or the Pokémon isn't holding its own Mega Stone.
- One of your other Pokémon (party or PC) is still in Mega form. Only 1 at a time.
- The battle doesn't allow Mega. In the Battle Tower, some floors from 15 up ban it (check the label before you start), and from floor 150 all gimmicks are banned. See [Features](/en/features/).

Ranked doubles (Regulation H) allow Mega and Tera. Ranked singles has its own rules, listed in the in-game RULES tab.

## How to Mega Evolve Rayquaza in Cobblemon

On Coco Cobblemon, Rayquaza doesn't use a Mega Stone. It has to **know Dragon Ascent**; without it you get "Rayquaza doesn't have dragonascent". Everything else is the same: wear a Mega Bracelet, start a battle, press the Mega button.

## Which Pokémon can Mega Evolve

Plain Cobblemon 1.7.3 only has data for 48 Mega forms across 46 species, and no way to use them. Mega Showdown, which Coco Cobblemon runs, has 81 Mega Stones for **78 species** (Charizard, Mewtwo and Raichu each have an X and a Y), plus Rayquaza through Dragon Ascent:

Abomasnow, Absol, Aerodactyl, Aggron, Alakazam, Altaria, Ampharos, Audino, Banette, Barbaracle, Beedrill, Blastoise, Blaziken, Camerupt, Chandelure, Charizard (X, Y), Chesnaught, Chimecho, Clefable, Crabominable, Delphox, Diancie, Dragalge, Dragonite, Drampa, Eelektross, Emboar, Excadrill, Falinks, Feraligatr, Floette, Froslass, Gallade, Garchomp, Gardevoir, Gengar, Glalie, Glimmora, Golurk, Greninja, Gyarados, Hawlucha, Heracross, Houndoom, Kangaskhan, Latias, Latios, Lopunny, Lucario, Malamar, Manectric, Mawile, Medicham, Meganium, Meowstic, Metagross, Mewtwo (X, Y), Pidgeot, Pinsir, Pyroar, Raichu (X, Y), Sableye, Salamence, Sceptile, Scizor, Scolipede, Scovillain, Scrafty, Sharpedo, Skarmory, Slowbro, Staraptor, Starmie, Steelix, Swampert, Tyranitar, Venusaur, Victreebel.

## Quick answers

### Can you Mega Evolve in Cobblemon?

Not in plain Cobblemon. On Coco Cobblemon (Mega Showdown mod), yes: Mega Bracelet on, Mega Stone held, then the Mega button in battle.

### How do you get Mega Rayquaza?

Teach Rayquaza Dragon Ascent. It doesn't need a stone, just the Mega Bracelet and the Mega button in battle.

### Where do you buy Mega Stones on Coco Cobblemon?

Shop Tokens: Menu (`/menu`) > Shop > Shop Tokens. You earn Tokens from the daily Battle Pass and expedition quests.
