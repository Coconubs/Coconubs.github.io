# How to evolve Pokémon in Cobblemon

In Cobblemon 1.7.3 a Pokémon that meets a level-up evolution condition does not change on its own: the game says "**(Pokémon) seems ready to evolve!**", and you open the **Summary** (M key), pick that Pokémon and press **Evolve**. Most species evolve by reaching a level. The rest need an evolution stone (hold the stone and right-click your Pokémon while it is sent out), a trade with another player or a **Link Cable**, friendship of at least 160, a time of day, or a held item.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

## Evolution methods in Cobblemon 1.7.3

Counted on 11 November 2026 from `Cobblemon-fabric-1.7.3+1.21.1.jar` (folder `data/cobblemon/species`): the 851 finished species declare 574 evolutions.

| Method | Evolutions | Example | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level up | 492 | Bulbasaur into Ivysaur at level 16 | Level the Pokémon up, then press **Evolve** in the Summary. Some species need an extra condition, see below |
| Use an item | 56 | Eevee with a Thunder Stone into Jolteon | Send the Pokémon out (R key), hold the item, right-click the Pokémon |
| Trade | 26 | Kadabra into Alakazam | Trade with another player, or use a Link Cable |

Evolution items only work on your own Pokémon. When a Pokémon qualifies for more than one evolution at the same time (Eevee, for example), the Evolve screen lets you choose.

## Evolution stones

Cobblemon 1.7.3 has 10 evolution stones: Fire Stone, Water Stone, Thunder Stone, Leaf Stone, Ice Stone, Moon Stone, Sun Stone, Dusk Stone, Dawn Stone and Shiny Stone. Thunder Stone and Leaf Stone are used the most (7 evolutions each), then Water Stone (6).

Each stone has its own ore in the world, for example Thunder Stone Ore; mine the ore to get the stone.

## Special conditions

| Condition | Examples in Cobblemon 1.7.3 |
|---|---|
| Friendship of 160 or more | Pichu into Pikachu, Golbat into Crobat, Chansey into Blissey, Munchlax into Snorlax |
| Friendship and time of day | Riolu into Lucario (day), Budew into Roselia (day), Eevee into Espeon (day) or Umbreon (night) |
| Held item and time of day | Gligar holding a Razor Fang at night into Gliscor; Sneasel holding a Razor Claw at night into Weavile; Happiny holding an Oval Stone during the day into Chansey |
| Knowing a move | Lickitung knowing Rollout into Lickilicky; Tangela knowing Ancient Power into Tangrowth; Aipom knowing Double Hit into Ambipom |
| Gender and a stone | Male Kirlia with a Dawn Stone into Gallade; female Snorunt with a Dawn Stone into Froslass |
| Another Pokémon in the party | Mantyke with a Remoraid in the party into Mantine; Pancham at level 32 or higher with a Dark-type Pokémon in the party into Pangoro |
| Weather | Sliggoo at level 50 or higher, levelling up while it rains, into Goodra |
| Distance | Pawmo, Bramblin and Rellor after 1,000 blocks travelled into Pawmot, Brambleghast and Rabsca |
| Using a move many times | Primeape using Rage Fist 20 times into Annihilape |
| Defeating Pokémon | Bisharp that defeats 3 opposing Bisharp holding a King's Rock evolves into Kingambit |
| Full moon | Ursaring with a Peat Block at night during a full moon into Ursaluna |

Friendship in Cobblemon goes up to 255; the species above need 160 or more.

## Trade evolutions and the Link Cable

26 evolutions need a trade. 8 species only need the trade: Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler, Haunter, Boldore, Gurdurr, Phantump and Pumpkaboo. 16 evolutions also need a held item during the trade, for example Onix holding a Metal Coat into Steelix, Scyther holding a Metal Coat into Scizor, Seadra holding a Dragon Scale into Kingdra, Porygon holding an Upgrade into Porygon2. Karrablast and Shelmet evolve when traded for each other.

**Trading with another player:** look at the player, press R and choose **Trade** on the interaction wheel. On the Coco Cobblemon server the two players must be within 10 blocks (the server's `tradeMaxDistance` setting, checked 10 November 2026).

**Playing alone, use a Link Cable.** It is a standard Cobblemon item; its tooltip reads "Evolves pokemon that require trading". Hold the Link Cable and right-click your Pokémon while it is sent out. The other conditions still apply: Onix still has to hold a Metal Coat. Karrablast needs a real trade with a Shelmet; Shelmet can use a Link Cable if a Karrablast is in your party.

Link Cable recipe (crafting table, 3x3 grid, from the Cobblemon 1.7.3 mod file):

| Column 1 | Column 2 | Column 3 |
|---|---|---|
| | | Nether Quartz |
| Copper Ingot | Ender Pearl | Copper Ingot |
| Nether Quartz | | |

You need 2 Nether Quartz, 2 Copper Ingots and 1 Ender Pearl. More recipes: [Cobblemon recipes](/en/cobblemon-recipes/).

## How to evolve Eevee in Cobblemon

Eevee has 8 evolutions in Cobblemon 1.7.3. Evolving Eevee into all 8 unlocks the advancement **Valued Veevees**.

| Evolution | How |
|---|---|
| Vaporeon | Use a Water Stone |
| Jolteon | Use a Thunder Stone |
| Flareon | Use a Fire Stone |
| Leafeon | Use a Leaf Stone |
| Glaceon | Use an Ice Stone |
| Espeon | Friendship 160 or more, level up during the day |
| Umbreon | Friendship 160 or more, level up at night |
| Sylveon | Friendship 160 or more, knows at least 1 Fairy-type move, level up |

In Cobblemon, Leafeon and Glaceon use a Leaf Stone and an Ice Stone; there is no Moss Rock or Ice Rock to find.

## Mega Evolution

Cobblemon 1.7.3 on its own cannot Mega Evolve: the mod file has Mega form data but no Mega Bracelet and no Mega Stones. The Coco Cobblemon server has Mega Evolution through the Mega Showdown mod; the steps, Mega Rayquaza and the species list are on [How to Mega Evolve in Cobblemon](/en/cobblemon-mega/).

## On the Coco Cobblemon server

Open the Menu (`/menu`) and choose **POKÉDEX** in the top bar to see how each species evolves and where it spawns, with a 3D model, types, stats and moves. A Pokémon wearing a costume that evolves into a species that cannot wear it gets the costume sent back to your wardrobe.

## Quick answers

### Why does my Pokémon not evolve at the right level?

In Cobblemon a Pokémon that qualifies is only marked as ready to evolve. Open the Summary (M key), pick the Pokémon and press **Evolve**. If there is no button, the species needs another condition, such as friendship, a time of day or a held item.

### How do I evolve trade Pokémon in single player?

In Cobblemon, use a Link Cable: craft it from 2 Nether Quartz, 2 Copper Ingots and 1 Ender Pearl, hold it and right-click the Pokémon. Pokémon that need a held item (Onix with a Metal Coat, for example) still need to hold it.

### How do I evolve Eevee into Sylveon in Cobblemon?

In Cobblemon 1.7.3, Eevee needs friendship of 160 or more and at least 1 Fairy-type move, then a level up; press Evolve in the Summary.

### How do you trade Pokémon in Cobblemon?

In Cobblemon, look at the other player, press R and choose **Trade** on the interaction wheel. Once they accept the request, the trade screen opens and both of you pick a Pokémon. On Coco Cobblemon the two players must be within 10 blocks; to sell Pokémon to anyone on the server, use the GTS market (`/gts`).

### Does Cobblemon have Mega Evolution?

Not in Cobblemon 1.7.3 on its own. The Coco Cobblemon server has Mega through the Mega Showdown mod: wear a Mega Bracelet, give the Pokémon its Mega Stone and press the Mega button in battle. See [How to Mega Evolve](/en/cobblemon-mega/).
