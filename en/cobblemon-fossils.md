# Cobblemon fossils: where to find them and how to revive fossil Pokémon

In Cobblemon 1.7.3, fossils are buried in **Suspicious Sand** and **Suspicious Gravel** at fossil sites; use a **Brush** to dig them out. To revive one, put a **Data Monitor** on top of a **Fossil Analyzer**, place a **Restoration Tank** next to it, insert the fossil, add 128 points of organic material and wait 12 minutes. Then right-click the machine with a Poké Ball to collect the Pokémon.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

## Cobblemon 1.7.3 fossil list

Cobblemon 1.7.3 has 15 fossil items and 15 revival recipes. 11 fossils work on their own. The 4 Galar fossils must be paired: a Fossilized Bird or Fossilized Fish with a Fossilized Drake or Fossilized Dino (see the table).

A revived Pokémon can get the **Revival Mark**.

## Where to find fossils

Cobblemon 1.7.3 adds 23 kinds of fossil site that generate in the world. Each site has some **Suspicious Sand** or **Suspicious Gravel**. Hold a **Brush** and hold **right-click** on the block to brush out the item.

- Every site has 1 block that always gives 1 fossil, as listed below.
- Up to 5 more blocks can give another fossil (which ones depends on the site), an Everstone, a Damp Rock, a Heat Rock or another held item.
- The other blocks give Bones, raw ores, coal, roots, herbs and evolution stones.

## How to build the machine and revive a fossil

1. Craft a **Fossil Analyzer**, a **Data Monitor** and a **Restoration Tank** (recipes below).
2. Place the Fossil Analyzer, then place the Data Monitor directly on top of it.
3. Place the Restoration Tank against one side of the Fossil Analyzer. The tank is 2 blocks tall. Once all 3 blocks are in place, the machine assembles itself.
4. Hold a fossil and **right-click** the machine to insert it. Galar fossils need both pieces.
5. Hold organic material and **right-click** the machine, one item per click, until it reaches **128 points**. With a fossil inside and 128 points, the machine starts on its own.
6. Wait **12 minutes**. When it is done, hold a **Poké Ball** and right-click the machine. The Pokémon joins your party in the ball you used.

- You can right-click any of the 3 blocks.
- Before the machine starts, right-click with an empty hand to take back the last fossil you inserted.
- While the machine is running, you cannot add fossils or organic material.
- For the first 5 minutes after the revival, only the player who inserted the fossil can collect the Pokémon; others see "This fossil belongs to [name]!". After 5 minutes, anyone with a Poké Ball can collect it.

The whole machine takes 10 Iron Ingots, 2 Copper Ingots, 6 Glass, 4 Cobbled Deepslate, 1 Amethyst Shard, 1 Redstone Dust, 1 Redstone Torch, 1 Revive and 1 Composter.

## Organic material: 128 points

Organic material means food, plants and things that come from living creatures. Cobblemon 1.7.3 lists 176 entries (134 from Minecraft, 42 from Cobblemon); some entries cover a whole group, such as every flower, every kind of leaves or every Berry. Each item is worth a set number of points:

| Points per item | Examples |
|---|---|
| 1 | Wheat Seeds, Dried Kelp, Melon Slice, Paper, dyes |
| 2 | Wheat, Sugar Cane, Sweet Berries, Kelp, Sugar, Cookie, flowers, leaves, saplings, Berries, Apricorns |
| 4 | Carrot, Potato, Beetroot, Apple, Bread, Bone Meal, Spider Eye, cooked meat and fish |
| 8 | Egg, Leather, Rotten Flesh, Pumpkin, Melon, Slimeball, Pumpkin Pie, Milk Bucket, raw meat and fish |
| 16 | Hay Bale, Cake, Rabbit Stew, Beetroot Soup, Honey Block, Sponge, Turtle Egg |
| 32 | Golden Apple, Ghast Tear, Bee Nest, Revive, Miracle Seed |
| 64 | Nautilus Shell, Dragon's Breath, Sniffer Egg, Max Revive |
| 256 | Enchanted Golden Apple, Nether Star, Dragon Egg |

- Quick fill: 8 **Hay Bales** make exactly 128 points (16 points each).
- The machine stops at 128 points. Extra points are lost, so do not feed it valuable items when it is almost full.
- Items in a bowl, bucket or bottle give the bowl, bucket or bottle back.
- You can add organic material before or after inserting the fossil.

## Reviving fossils on the Coco Cobblemon server

- **Normal fossils are free:** the 15 Pokémon in the table above are not on the server's fee list, so they revive just like in plain Cobblemon (server config checked 11 November 2026).
- **Finding fossils works as in the base mod:** the COBBLEVERSE data pack on the server does not change Cobblemon's fossil sites or brushing loot.
- **Mewtwo and Type: Null can be revived:** the COBBLEVERSE data pack adds 2 machine recipes: **Ancient DNA** + **Cloning Catalyst** gives Mewtwo, and **Fossilized Helmet** gives Type: Null.
- **Collecting Mewtwo or Type: Null costs 20,000,000$** (CobbleDollars) each. The server shows the fee and asks you to confirm before taking the money. Without enough money you cannot collect it.
- **You cannot break the machine while it holds Mewtwo or Type: Null:** collect the Pokémon with a Poké Ball first.

## Quick answers

### Where do you find fossils in Cobblemon?

In Cobblemon, fossils are in Suspicious Sand and Suspicious Gravel at 23 kinds of fossil site: deserts, plains, forests, swamps, jungles, badlands, snowy and icy areas, caves and the sea floor. Use a Brush to dig them out.

### How do you revive a fossil in Cobblemon?

Put a Data Monitor on a Fossil Analyzer and place a Restoration Tank next to it. Insert the fossil, add 128 points of organic material, wait 12 minutes, then right-click the machine with a Poké Ball.

### What do you need to build the Cobblemon fossil machine?

Three blocks: a Fossil Analyzer, a Data Monitor and a Restoration Tank. Together they take 10 Iron Ingots, 2 Copper Ingots, 6 Glass, 4 Cobbled Deepslate, 1 Amethyst Shard, 1 Redstone Dust, 1 Redstone Torch, 1 Revive and 1 Composter.

### What counts as organic material in Cobblemon?

In Cobblemon, organic material is food, plants and items from living creatures, each worth a set number of points. The machine needs 128 points, for example 8 Hay Bales.

### Does reviving fossils cost money on Coco Cobblemon?

Normal fossils such as Omanyte, Kabuto, Aerodactyl and Tyrunt are free. On Coco Cobblemon, collecting Mewtwo or Type: Null from the machine costs 20,000,000$ in CobbleDollars.

### Can you revive Mewtwo in Cobblemon?

Plain Cobblemon 1.7.3 has no such recipe. On Coco Cobblemon, the COBBLEVERSE data pack adds Ancient DNA + Cloning Catalyst for Mewtwo, with a 20,000,000$ fee.
