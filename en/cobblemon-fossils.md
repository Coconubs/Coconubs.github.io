# Cobblemon fossils: where to find them and how to revive fossil Pokémon

In Cobblemon 1.7.3, fossils are buried in **Suspicious Sand** and **Suspicious Gravel** at fossil sites. Dig them out with a **Brush**. To revive one, stack a **Data Monitor** on a **Fossil Analyzer**, put a **Restoration Tank** next to it, insert the fossil, add 128 points of organic material and wait 12 minutes. Then right-click the machine with a Poké Ball to grab your Pokémon.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

## Where to find fossils

Cobblemon 1.7.3 adds 23 kinds of fossil site to world generation. Each one has some **Suspicious Sand** or **Suspicious Gravel**. Hold a **Brush** and hold **right-click** on the block to brush it out.

- Every site has 1 block that always drops 1 fossil (listed below).
- Up to 5 more blocks can drop another fossil (depends on the site), an Everstone, a Damp Rock, a Heat Rock or some other held item.
- The rest give Bones, raw ores, coal, roots, herbs and evolution stones.

## How to build the machine and revive a fossil

1. Craft a **Fossil Analyzer**, a **Data Monitor** and a **Restoration Tank** (recipes below).
2. Place the Fossil Analyzer and put the Data Monitor right on top of it.
3. Place the Restoration Tank against one side of the Fossil Analyzer. The tank is 2 blocks tall. Once all 3 blocks are down, the machine forms by itself.
4. Hold a fossil and **right-click** the machine to put it in. Galar fossils need both halves.
5. Hold organic material and **right-click** the machine, one item per click, until it hits **128 points**. With a fossil inside and 128 points, it starts by itself.
6. Wait **12 minutes**. Then hold a **Poké Ball** and right-click the machine. The Pokémon joins your party in that ball.

- You can right-click any of the 3 blocks.
- Before it starts, right-click with an empty hand to take back the last fossil you put in.
- Once it's running, you can't add fossils or organic material.
- For 5 minutes after the revival, only the player who put the fossil in can collect the Pokémon; everyone else sees "This fossil belongs to [name]!". After that, anyone with a Poké Ball can take it.

## Organic material: 128 points

Organic material is food, plants and stuff that comes from living things. Cobblemon 1.7.3 lists 176 entries (134 from Minecraft, 42 from Cobblemon), and some entries cover a whole group, like all flowers, all leaves or all Berries. Each item is worth a fixed number of points:

| Points per item | Examples |
|---|---|
| 1 | Wheat Seeds, Dried Kelp, Melon Slice, Paper, dyes |
| 2 | Wheat, Sugar Cane, Sweet Berries, Kelp, Sugar, Cookie, flowers, leaves, saplings, Berries, Apricorns |
| 4 | Carrot, Potato, Beetroot, Apple, Bread, Bone Meal, Spider Eye, cooked meat and fish |
| 8 | Egg, Leather, Rotten Flesh, Pumpkin, Melon, Slimeball, Pumpkin Pie, Milk Bucket, raw meat and fish |
| 16 | Hay Bale, Cake, Rabbit Stew, Beetroot Soup, Honey Block, Sponge, Turtle Egg |
| 32 | Golden Apple, Ghast Tear, Bee Nest, Revive, Miracle Seed |
| 64 | Nautilus Shell, Dragon's Breath, Sniffer Egg, Max Revive |
| 256 | Enchanted Golden Apple, Nether Star, Dragon Egg |

- Fastest fill: 8 **Hay Bales** = exactly 128 points (16 each).
- It caps at 128. Anything over is wasted, so don't throw in valuable stuff when it's nearly full.
- Bowls, buckets and bottles come back to you.
- You can add organic material before or after the fossil.

## Reviving fossils on the Coco Cobblemon server

- **Normal fossils are free:** the 15 Pokémon above aren't on the server's fee list, so they revive just like in plain Cobblemon (server config checked 11 November 2026).
- **Finding fossils is the same as the base mod:** the server's COBBLEVERSE data pack doesn't change fossil sites or brushing loot.
- **You can revive Mewtwo and Type: Null:** COBBLEVERSE adds 2 machine recipes. **Ancient DNA** + **Cloning Catalyst** gives Mewtwo, and **Fossilized Helmet** gives Type: Null.
- **Collecting Mewtwo or Type: Null costs 20,000,000$** (CobbleDollars) each. The server shows the price and asks you to confirm first. If you can't afford it, you can't collect it.
- **You can't break the machine while Mewtwo or Type: Null is inside.** Collect it with a Poké Ball first.

## Quick answers

### Where do you find fossils in Cobblemon?

In Suspicious Sand and Suspicious Gravel at 23 kinds of fossil site: deserts, plains, forests, swamps, jungles, badlands, snowy and icy areas, caves and the sea floor. Brush them out.

### How do you revive a fossil in Cobblemon?

Data Monitor on top of a Fossil Analyzer, Restoration Tank next to it. Put the fossil in, add 128 points of organic material, wait 12 minutes and right-click with a Poké Ball.

### What counts as organic material?

Food, plants and things from living creatures, each worth a set number of points. You need 128, which is 8 Hay Bales.

### Does reviving fossils cost money on Coco Cobblemon?

Normal fossils like Omanyte, Kabuto, Aerodactyl and Tyrunt are free. Mewtwo and Type: Null cost 20,000,000$ (CobbleDollars) to collect.

### Can you revive Mewtwo in Cobblemon?

Not in plain Cobblemon 1.7.3. On Coco Cobblemon, the COBBLEVERSE data pack adds Ancient DNA + Cloning Catalyst for Mewtwo, with a 20,000,000$ fee.
