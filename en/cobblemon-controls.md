# Cobblemon controls

Cobblemon 1.7.3 adds 6 keybinds to Minecraft Java Edition. By default, **R** sends out your selected Pokémon and starts battles, **M** opens the Summary, **O** hides the party overlay, the **Up and Down arrows** switch between party Pokémon, and **Left Alt** lets you look around while riding. To throw a Poké Ball, hold it and right-click. You can rebind them in **Options** > **Controls** > **Key Binds**, under **Cobblemon**.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

## Default keybinds

| Key | Name in the Controls menu | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| R | Throw Selected Pokémon | Sends out your selected Pokémon or calls it back. Look at a wild Pokémon close by and press R to start a battle. Look at another player and press R to open the interaction menu (battle request, trade). In a battle, it shrinks or reopens the battle screen. While riding, it gets you off |
| M | Open Summary | Opens the party Summary: info, moves and stats for each Pokémon |
| O | Hide Party Overlay | Hides or shows the party column on the left of the screen |
| Up arrow, Down arrow | Party Shift Up, Party Shift Down | Moves the selection up or down your party. Whoever is selected is who R sends out |
| Left Alt | Riding Freelook | Look around freely while riding. See [How to ride Pokémon in Cobblemon](/en/cobblemon-riding/) |

Everything else is normal Minecraft: right-click (**Use Item/Place Block**) throws the Poké Ball in your hand, and left-click (**Attack/Destroy**) does what it always does.

Read from `Cobblemon-fabric-1.7.3+1.21.1.jar` (default key codes and names in `en_us.json`) on 11 November 2026. Other versions may differ.

## Changing a keybind

1. On the title screen, or in game after pressing Esc, click **Options...**.
2. Click **Controls...**, then **Key Binds...**.
3. Scroll down to **Cobblemon**, click the key you want to change and press the new one. Clashing keys turn red.
4. Click **Done**.

If R doesn't send out your Pokémon, check whether R shows up red under Cobblemon. That means something else is using it.

## On the Coco Cobblemon server

On top of the Cobblemon keys, Coco Cobblemon has its own menu: type `/menu`, or hold the **Menu** item in the last hotbar slot (slot 9) and right-click. To switch it to English, use the VI/EN flag or `/ngonngu en`. See [How to join](/en/how-to-join/).

## Quick answers

### What key sends out a Pokémon in Cobblemon?

R (Throw Selected Pokémon). Pick one with the Up and Down arrows, press R to send it out, and R again to call it back.

### How do I start a battle with a wild Pokémon?

Look at it from close by and press R. Your selected Pokémon comes out and the battle starts. If you'd rather just throw a ball, hold a Poké Ball and right-click.

### Where do I change Cobblemon keybinds?

**Options** > **Controls** > **Key Binds**, then scroll down to **Cobblemon**.
