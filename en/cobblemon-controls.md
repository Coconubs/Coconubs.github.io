# Cobblemon controls

Cobblemon 1.7.3 adds 6 keybinds to Minecraft Java Edition. By default: **R** sends out your selected Pokémon and starts battles, **M** opens the Summary, **O** hides the party overlay, the **Up and Down arrow keys** pick a Pokémon in your party, and **Left Alt** lets you look around while riding. To throw a Poké Ball, hold it and right-click. Change keys in **Options** > **Controls** > **Key Binds**, under the **Cobblemon** category.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

## Default keybinds

| Key | Name in the Controls menu | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| R | Throw Selected Pokémon | Sends out your selected Pokémon or calls it back. Look at a nearby wild Pokémon and press R to start a battle. Look at another player and press R to open the interaction menu for that player (battle request, trade). During a battle: minimise or reopen the battle screen. While riding a Pokémon: dismount |
| M | Open Summary | Opens the party Summary screen: info, moves and stats of each Pokémon |
| O | Hide Party Overlay | Hides or shows the party column on the left of the screen |
| Up arrow, Down arrow | Party Shift Up, Party Shift Down | Selects the Pokémon above or below in your party. The selected Pokémon is the one R sends out |
| Left Alt | Riding Freelook | While riding a Pokémon: look around freely |

Everything else uses the normal Minecraft keys: right-click (**Use Item/Place Block**) throws the Poké Ball in your hand, left-click (**Attack/Destroy**) works as usual.

This table was read from `Cobblemon-fabric-1.7.3+1.21.1.jar` (default key codes and key names in `en_us.json`) on 11 November 2026. Other Cobblemon versions may differ.

## Change a keybind

1. From the title screen, or in game after pressing Esc, choose **Options...**.
2. Choose **Controls...**, then **Key Binds...**.
3. Scroll down to the **Cobblemon** category, click the key you want to change and press the new key. Conflicting keys are shown in red.
4. Press **Done**.

If R does not send out your Pokémon, check whether R is shown in red under the Cobblemon category, which means another key uses it.

## On the Coco Cobblemon server

Besides the Cobblemon keys, the Coco Cobblemon server has its own menu: type `/menu`, or hold the **Menu** item in the last hotbar slot (slot 9) and right-click. Switch the menu to English with the VI/EN flag or `/ngonngu en`. See [How to join](/en/how-to-join/).

## Quick answers

### Which key sends out a Pokémon in Cobblemon?

R (Throw Selected Pokémon). Pick a Pokémon with the Up and Down arrow keys, press R to send it out, and press R again to call it back.

### How do I start a battle with a wild Pokémon?

Look at a nearby wild Pokémon and press R: your selected party Pokémon comes out to battle. To catch without battling, hold a Poké Ball and right-click to throw it.

### Where do I change Cobblemon keybinds?

**Options** > **Controls** > **Key Binds**, then scroll down to the **Cobblemon** category.
